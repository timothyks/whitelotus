Drive Team Builder

Gaby enters her sixth year in robotics. She began her robotics experience in fourth grade competing in VEXIQ for two years. She attended Lake Ridge Middle School and competed in VEXV5 for three years. In eighth grade she achieved her goal of leading her team to Worlds.
