About Us

We are 3760X White Lotus, a private VEX Robotics team based in Woodbridge, Virginia. Founded in 2026, we are dedicated to designing and building competitive VEX V5 robots.

Our team is composed of passionate high school students from across Prince William County, who are committed to learning and excelling in the field of robotics. We participate in various competitions and strive to innovate and improve our designs each season.
